# Blue Fit: selling points

Features worth selling. Add one line each time something stands out. Details live in `APP_SPEC.md`.

## The headline: Build my plan
- **A complete, research-based plan in under a minute.** Answer a few questions (goal, eating, days, workout length, rest between sets, which muscles to prioritize) and the app builds your whole week. It picks the right split for your days and the top exercises for your goal. It covers every angle of every muscle and includes stretch exercises. It gives each muscle its rest days, keeps every workout within your time, spreads the sets evenly across days and hits every weekly target. Before building, it shows what can't fit and offers one-tap fixes. Then you can swap any exercise for another that works the same muscle and angle, remove exercises, add your own, or build again for a different plan. Every plan is checked against the Plan check and scored. (§5.20b)

## Smart training
- **Automatic "last set to failure".** The app picks which exercises go to failure in each workout, based on the whole plan and on what you actually did this week (skipped workouts and exercises included). It considers your goal, recovery and weekly targets. Tap the flame to see why. (§5.19)
- **Personal weekly volume targets.** Sets per muscle come from your goal, age, sleep, diet phase and training years, and you can set each muscle to Priority, Normal or Skip. (§5.17)
- **Deload suggestions.** The app watches every exercise (weight and reps) and suggests a lighter week when half of them stall. (§5.16)
- **Plan score.** One number for how good your week is: volume, every part of every muscle, recovery, failure in the right places, and not too much in one workout. Tap it to see what to fix. (§5.21)
- **Your week on a body map.** Every muscle colored by whether the plan hits its weekly target; tap one to zoom in and see each part (front/side/rear delts, both biceps heads and the brachialis…) and whether your plan trains it. (§5.21)
- **Progression hints.** Each exercise tells you when to add weight, and only once you've proven you're ready, not after one lucky day. (§5.5)
- **Built-in strength test.** When you first reach the top of your reps, the app quietly turns that exercise's last set into a test to failure at the right moment (when it's safe and you're recovered), so it knows whether to add weight next time. (§5.19)

## Logging
- **Fast logger.** Weights and reps are pre-filled from last time, the rest timer starts automatically, and supersets are supported. (§3.3, §5.6, §5.7)
- **Plates per side.** The app shows which plates to load. (§5.9)
- **Animated line-man figures** show how to do every exercise. (§3.5)

## Progress
- **Fractional weekly volume.** A direct set counts 1 and an indirect set counts ½, per muscle. (§5.2)
- **Muscle recovery map.** (§5.13)
- **Records and progress charts.** (§5.4, §3.11)
