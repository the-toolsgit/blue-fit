Chin-Up ★

Bodyweightkey liftfailure okBiceps ½

Palms-up pull-up: back plus a lot of biceps.

Setup

  • Palms facing you, hands shoulder-width.
  • Hang with arms straight.

Form

  • Pull up until your chin is over the bar.
  • Lower until your arms are straight.

Avoid

  • Kicking or swinging.
  • Half reps at the top.
