Weekly sets: sets that hit the muscle only partly (like biceps in a row) count as half. Goals: Mass gain grow muscle. Most sets 6–30 reps, a couple short of failure, rests 60 s+. Strength lift heavier. Key lifts at 6 reps or less, rests 3 min+, fewer total sets. Endurance last longer. Most exercises 15+ reps, rests 90 s or less. Maintain keep what you have with less time. About a third of your growth sets, kept heavy (Bickel 2011

How your profile changes sets goal

Each muscle's weekly range below is for your goal. Your profile then scales it: the changes multiply together, the range is rounded, and the top always stays at least 2 above the bottom. Your own targets always win. our call
